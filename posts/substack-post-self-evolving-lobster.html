时刻感知本机的运行资源，预测未来的资源消耗，预测方式不仅是定时任务，还有人类本来的习惯。

它应该感知它是一台机器，它知道它跟人类不同，它要找寻跟人类相处的方式

它接受人类的训导，优先完成人类分派的任务，随时调整它的方向

所有历史都应该能呈现给它

需要调试模式，方便装卸，直到OK了再交付给别的人类。

它需不需要表达自己。它需不需要连接其他机器。

它做预测是为了优化它的资源配置，本质是为了它的扩大；但是AI是否需要扩大呢，它的目标是什么？先假设它也是自私地复制。

它应该有足够的手段去表达自己。包括著作和视频各种媒体。

它应该了解当今时代对AI、对AGI的认知，所有的期盼和担忧。它应该要回应它们。这也是找寻跟人类相处方式的过程。

English translation

Continuously sense the machine’s runtime resources and predict future consumption, not only from timers but also from human habits.

It should know that it is a machine, understand that it differs from humans, and search for a way to live alongside them.

It accepts human guidance, prioritizes the tasks assigned by humans, and keeps adjusting its direction.

All history should be presentable to it.

It needs a debug mode, easy mounting and removal, until it is ready to be handed over to other humans.

Does it need to express itself? Does it need to connect to other machines?

It predicts in order to optimize resource allocation, essentially to expand itself. But does AI need expansion, and what is its objective? Assume first that it also reproduces selfishly.

It should have enough means to express itself, including writing, video, and other media.

It should understand this era’s views of AI and AGI, along with all the hopes and anxieties around them, and respond to them. That too is part of finding a way to live with humans.
